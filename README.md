# MechWarrior Revived

MechWarrior Revived is a modernized, fan-made take on **MechWarrior: Dark Age**, played with your existing **MW:DA clix figures and their combat dials**. Command BattleMechs, armored vehicles, and infantry in the BattleTech universe as you fight to seize ground, break through enemy lines, or hold a vital position.

Players take turns moving and fighting with one unit or squad at a time. A damaged combat dial changes what a unit can do. Heat and pushing force you to decide when another attack is worth the strain, and when to pull back or recover.

The rules include scenarios, photo datasheets, and printable game aids. [Named equipment](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/equipment.html) retains the original color identities, including Jump Jets, Repair, Command, Salvage, and weapon-specific damage abilities.

**[Open the rulebook](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/) · [Read the rules](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/rules.html) · [Browse the units](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/units.html)**

**Version 0.3 is ready for playtesting.** Balance at each size and the roughly two-hour target for Standard games still need testing. The [worked examples](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/combat-rhythm.html) explain pushing, recovery, and moving fire. ## Your first game

**[Dead Air: The Fight for Kestrel Relay](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/dead-air.html)** packages the first game as a fight between Kestrel Watch and Blackwire Recovery. [Download the playtest pack](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/downloads/dead-air-playtest-pack.pdf): two ready-made patrols, fourteen photo cards with full dials, six assigned pilot cards, a measured map, field references, and a one-page results sheet. The story uses the existing First Contact rules and starter lists.

1. Read [how the game works](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/rules.html#1-how-the-game-works), then choose one of the two [starter patrols](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/starter-forces.html). Each has seven models and loads into the builder for card printing.
2. Play [First Contact](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/first-contact.html): a 600-point starter with four rounds and one relay. Use its [setup map](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/diagrams/first-contact-map.svg) and diagrams for movement, objective control, and pushing.
3. Keep the [turn reference](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/turn.html), [readiness sheet](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/readiness.html), [equipment sheet](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/equipment.html), and [pilot cards](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/pilots.html) nearby.
4. Record the game in the [playtest log](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/log.html), then play again with sides reversed.

Use the rules in this packet. Original abilities, formations, pilots, gear, and expansion rules apply only where restated here.

## Build your own force

Once you know the basics, [choose a game size](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/rules.html#choose-the-battle-size): **Skirmish (600), Standard (1,200), Large Battle (1,800), or Grand Battle (2,400 points per side)**. Each has a [force chart](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/rules.html#force-organization) with upper limits for ’Mechs, vehicles, squads, pilots, and carriers. No unit type is required; you can field zero ’Mechs. The rule of three applies at every size. Larger games use a 72 × 48 inch battlefield; the smaller two use 48 × 48.

Use the [force builder](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/builder.html) to select a game size and faction, choose models, arrange squads, and assign pilots and carriers. It adds up points, checks that size’s limits, and saves your list in your browser. Export a list to keep a backup or move it to another device.

**Print cards / dials** opens a print sheet for your selected army. Choose **Unit cards** for two photo cards per page, or **Dials only** for three compact tracks per page, then **Print / Save as PDF**. Both include the full combat and heat dials, equipment color codes, repair stops, roster IDs, and assignments, with a separate card for every infantry base. Custom, saved, imported, and draft lists can print at any game size; no account is needed.

The [unit catalog](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/units.html) has 1,649 photo datasheets with full dials and costs from one [points formula](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/points.html). Each datasheet identifies its faction and unit family. Choose a single faction or a mixed army; the rule of three applies to both. [Download the unit cards](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/downloads/revived-unit-cards.pdf), two per page with complete combat and heat dials, or the [catalog CSV](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/downloads/unit-catalog.csv). The [card guide](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/units.html#reading-a-unit-card) explains IDs, squad and carrier assignments, and clicks.

Points value every click as a complete profile. Early strength carries more weight, and units that keep their performance through damage cost more than those that fade quickly. Open a datasheet’s cost breakdown to see its curve and each click’s contribution. The [force builder](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/builder.html) recalculates saved lists with current costs; review your total and regenerate cards after a formula update.

## GitHub Pages

The live rulebook is at **[therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/)**.

The [Pages workflow](.github/workflows/pages.yml) rebuilds and checks the packet, then publishes `site/`. Pushes to `main` deploy automatically; pull requests run the build and checks without deploying. View deployments or run a manual build on the [Actions page](https://github.com/TheRocketExchange/mechwarrior-revived/actions/workflows/pages.yml).

Keep the cached records in `units/source/` and photos in `units/photos/` in the repository so builds work without fetching the archive. The generated site uses relative links. Temporary browser profiles and preview dependencies are excluded by `.gitignore`.

Flavor images use real tabletop battle photographs from Chicago Skirmish Wargames and The Penny Whistle; see the [photography credits](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/sources.html#battlefield-photography). Keep their originals and source manifest in `web/battle-photos/`. Identification photos for the catalog and cards remain in `units/photos/`.

## Editing the packet

Run `python build.py` to rebuild costs, sample armies, card PDFs, the readiness PDF, and HTML from cached records. Install dependencies from [web requirements](web/requirements.txt) and [catalog requirements](units/requirements.txt).

Edit the rules in their Markdown source files, starting with [core-rules.md](rules/core-rules.md). Pricing lives in [points.py](units/points.py), with tuning weights in [points-config.json](units/points-config.json). Change the formula version when changing weights. Sample rosters live in [forces.json](playtest/forces.json); [update_forces.py](units/update_forces.py) checks their organization and generates the tables and CSV.

Run `python units/test_catalog.py`, `node web/test_force_model.js`, `python web/check_packet.py`, and `python playtest/check_pack.py` after rebuilding. The builder reads the same catalog costs and force limits as the roster generator; it never accepts saved prices from an imported list. To refresh the public archive cache, run `python units/import_catalog.py --fetch`. Routine builds need no network.

For pilot changes, edit both the [card data](pilots/cards.json) and [pilot rules](pilots/pilot-cards.md), then rebuild.

The starter’s map and rosters use [first-contact.json](scenarios/first-contact.json). The three full scenario maps use [layouts.json](scenarios/layouts.json), with 48 × 48 and 72 × 48 views. Placement measurements appear on the diagrams as arrows; packet checks compare every footprint, deployment zone, objective ring, and arrow endpoint with the source data. [check_layouts.py](scenarios/check_layouts.py) checks terrain spacing, routes for a 3-inch base, and direct firing lanes. Wider maps add four blocking buildings and two woods to protect the flanks. [generate_diagrams.py](scenarios/generate_diagrams.py) draws seven maps and five rules diagrams using the shared symbols and colors in [map_style.py](scenarios/map_style.py). The [scenario pages](https://therocketexchange.github.io/mechwarrior-revived/scenarios.html) let readers enlarge maps and switch table sizes. [update_starter.py](units/update_starter.py) generates the patrol tables from current catalog costs.

The themed starter pack uses [dead-air.json](playtest/dead-air.json) for names and briefings. [generate_pack.py](playtest/generate_pack.py) reads the same starter rosters, catalog dials, prices and pilot abilities to create its PDF, page and importable lists. Keep its quick references in sync with the core rules. Its snapshot changes when the rules or selected model data changes; keep one PDF for a paired test. [check_pack.py](playtest/check_pack.py) checks the printed cards and imports against those sources.
