# Turn reference

Version 0.3 · Keep the [readiness sheet](readiness-reference.md), [equipment sheet](equipment-reference.md), and [pilot cards](../pilots/pilot-cards.md) nearby.

## Each round

1. Clear activation markers only; keep readiness and heat. Roll one die each; reroll ties. Winner chooses who acts first.
2. Alternate one ’Mech, vehicle, or squad. Choose one action for the group; finish squad members separately, with their own targets and rolls.
3. Pass only with fewer groups left than your opponent. Pass automatically with none.
4. After everyone acts, score the scenario and check for victory. No round-end cooling.

Squads finish in a chain with neighboring centers at most 4 inches apart. Split squads regroup without attacking.

## Readiness: vehicles and squads

Start **Ready**. Declaring Fire, Advance, or Assault changes Ready → Committed → Spent. On the second combat order, each surviving base takes **1 unavoidable damage after its own action**, even if it cannot attack or misses. A surviving critical miss pays both costs.

**Spent must recover next activation.** Move or Hold clears readiness. Engaged bases Hold or Disengage; other squad members may Move or Hold. Normal Disengage costs apply. Completing a forced regroup also clears readiness, but never permits an exit.

Rush, Repair, Embark, and Disembark do not change readiness and are forbidden while Spent. Spent groups still count for passes and objectives. Recovery neither heals nor grants an extra activation. ’Mechs use heat instead.

## Actions

Speed = printed speed plus heat, at least zero. Round half speed up, then add equipment bonuses.

| Action | What you do | ’Mech heat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Stay still; ranged or close attack | +1 |
| Advance | Move up to half speed; ranged attack | +2 |
| Move | Move up to speed; no attack; non-’Mechs recover | +1 |
| Rush | Move up to twice speed; no attack | +1 |
| Assault | Infantry/’Mech: move up to half speed; close attack | +1 |
| Disengage | Take 1 unavoidable damage; move up to half speed; no attack | +1 |
| Hold | Stay still; no attack; non-’Mechs recover | Cool 1 |
| Vent | Stay still; no attack | Cool vent rating, at least 1 |
| Repair | Gray attack: heal 1 friendly click | +1 |

Turn only during movement actions. In enemy contact, use close Fire, Assault in place, Hold, Vent, or Disengage. Vehicles cannot Assault or fight close. Disengage causes no damage if every touching enemy is shutdown.

## Attacks

Choose one target and weapon. Check arc, sight, and range, including both endpoints.

**Three dice + attack + modifiers ≥ defense = hit.**

- White 6/6: automatic hit, +1 damage.
- White 1/1: automatic miss; take 1 unavoidable damage.
- Rear attack: +2 attack, except indirect fire.
- Shutdown target: −2 defense.
- Equipment: at most +1 attack and +1 defense.
- Advance: no extra attack penalty.

**Ballistic:** +1 damage against uncovered infantry. Moving or turning gives −1 attack; Gunner removes it during Advance within half speed, counting terrain.

**Energy:** Piercing; +1 heat for the firing ’Mech; damage against conventional infantry is limited to 2 before armor. No automatic target heat.

**Rocket:** Fire only. Indirect fire ignores blockers: −2 attack, −1 damage, no rear bonus. Keep arc, range, cover, and friendly-contact restrictions.

Weapon-type effects apply only to shooting.

**Damage:** Printed value + heat → weapon effect → equipment/pilot bonus (maximum +1), battle armor bonus, critical bonus → damage limits → armor after Piercing. Damage may be zero.

Against vehicles/’Mechs, conventional infantry deal at most 1 before armor. Exposed close assaults raise this to 2 with Piercing: rear contact, shutdown target, or attacker in woods/rubble/fieldworks. Battle armor gets +1 close damage and Piercing. Piercing ignores 1 armor; it does not add together.

Three bullet holes destroy a unit. Read new dials between attacks. Apply attacker heat or pushing damage last, including after a surviving critical miss.

## Terrain and heat

- **Woods/rubble:** Each inch costs 2. Ranged cover: +2 conventional infantry, +1 others.
- **Fieldworks:** Infantry only; normal movement. Ranged cover: +3 conventional, +2 battle armor. Conventional infantry also get +1 close defense.
- **Water:** Each inch costs 2, except hover. No cover or cooling.
- **Blocking terrain:** Stops movement and direct sight.

Use only the best cover bonus, up to +3.

Apply printed heat modifiers. After gaining heat, a colored warning other than green/white means one stress roll: on 1, take 1 unavoidable damage. No roll while cooling or shutdown.

Stop heating at shutdown. Only Vent is allowed; the ’Mech cannot contest objectives. Cooling past shutdown restarts it without another activation.

## Transport and objectives

**Board:** Spend the squad’s activation. Move up to speed; finish together, all centers within 3 inches of an empty friendly carrier. Neither may touch an enemy.

**Unload:** Spend the squad’s activation. Carrier must be unengaged. Place everyone together within 3 inches, legally and outside enemy contact. No further action. If all cannot fit, stay aboard with readiness unchanged. Carrier acts separately.

Track passenger readiness separately. Hold aboard clears it; boarding, unloading, or evacuation does not. A Spent squad must Hold before unloading on a later activation.

**Destroyed carrier:** Each passenger takes 1 unavoidable damage. Place survivors legally within 3 inches, outside enemy contact. Save as many as fit; the rest die. Mark the squad activated; regroup next round if split.

**Objectives:** Base centers within 3 inches count, including Spent units. Both sides present = contested. Shutdown, embarked, and escaped units do not count. Breakthrough permits exits with Move, Advance, or Rush; an on-foot squad leaves together. Regrouping cannot score an exit.
