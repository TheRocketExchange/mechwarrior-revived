## Visual references and fonts

The site's condensed type, heavy headings and pale field-sheet surfaces take their cues from the [original Dark Age rulebook](https://cf.sarna.net/docs/products/mwda/rule_book.pdf). The angular panels and metallic borders also draw on the [Age of Destruction equipment sheet](https://www.warrenborn.com/Files/Rules/MWAD_SEC_lo.pdf). Steel-blue headers, blue-gray panels and beveled controls recall early-2000s gaming forums. The navigation retains the influence of [NetEA](https://www.net-armageddon.org/).

The site bundles **Russo One** for headings, **Roboto Condensed** for reading and controls, and **Orbitron** for the Revived wordmark and selected numbers. These are visual alternatives, not the original proprietary fonts. Each comes from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, with the notices included in the download: [Russo One](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/russoone/OFL.txt), [Roboto Condensed](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/robotocondensed/OFL.txt), [Orbitron](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/orbitron/OFL.txt). All three load locally so the offline rulebook keeps its typography.

## What carries over

Use current dial values and printed ranges. Roll three dice plus attack against defense. The two white dice decide critical hits and misses. See Dark Age pp. 14–19.

Terrain bonuses, damage limits, heat timing, transports, pilots, and scoring in this packet are new Revived rules.

Death from Above retains Dark Age’s Jump requirement, unengaged start, non-infantry target, +2 target defense, +2 damage, and 1/2 landing damage on a hit/miss. Revived treats it as a close attack with its own action and 2 total heat; its normal equipment and armor apply. It grants no reactive pivot. Use the [Revived rule](../rules/core-rules.md#death-from-above), not the original timing or equipment exceptions.

## Using older figures

Equipment retains the original names and color assignments, with shorter Revived effects. Ballistic/Rocket, Energy, and Melee damage use separate tables. Purple Domination equipment is included; white, yellow, and unlisted combat colors have no effect. Original named heat effects and circle-use costs still do not apply.

Old symbols do not identify Rockets separately. Use fixed labels from an agreed source, or mark a Rocket test substitute. That label does not establish the figure’s original armament.

Every unit uses the same [points formula](../rules/points.md). Its weights are playtest estimates.

## Unit catalog and photographs

The catalog includes all **1,649 miniature entries** in WarrenBorn’s full index: 1,644 figures and five DropShip turret sections. The 712 separate pilot, gear, and other card entries are outside this miniature catalog. Each datasheet links to its exact archive record and photograph. Photos are locally cached so the cards work offline; they retain their source credit.

Each collector variant keeps its own dial, photo, and cost. Quad ’Mechs use ’Mech rules. Gray Death Scout Armor and PAL suits use battle armor rules. Aircraft, aquatic units, colossal ’Mechs, artillery-only units, and DropShip sections are included for catalog completeness and marked as outside the core rules.

For old Ballistic symbols, only an unambiguous single-weapon missile/LRM/SRM unit name identifies a Rocket. Other weapons remain Ballistic unless documented otherwise. Named equipment is shown on the datasheets at the selected click; unlisted combat colors give no effect.

Cached source pages, the import manifest, and the coverage report are in `units/`. This establishes coverage of the archive index; it is not an independent audit of every promotional release. Check an archive discrepancy against the physical dial before playing. The faction on each profile is the archive's allegiance for that exact figure; single-faction lists use that label without inferred allies.
