At the table / Readiness

Readiness reference

Version 0.3 · Vehicles and infantry squads · ’Mechs use heat instead.

Start Ready. Track one state per vehicle or whole squad, separately from its activation marker. Keep readiness between rounds. Print this sheet on US Letter paper.

Combat orders: Fire, Advance, Assault, Bypass

Before After declaring Pushing cost
Ready Committed None
Committed Spent Each surviving base takes 1 unavoidable damage after its action
Spent Must recover Cannot choose a combat order

One squad order; separate targets and rolls. Pay pushing damage even if a base skips its shot, misses, or cannot attack. Resolve critical-miss damage first, then push if still alive. Never pass a casualty’s damage to another base.

Recovery

Choose Move (full speed, no attack) or Hold (stay still). Clear readiness after the group finishes. An engaged base must Hold or Disengage; other squad members may Move or Hold. Disengage keeps its damage cost and half-speed limit. Keep the squad together.

Spent groups must recover next activation. Rush, Repair, Coolant Flush, Embark, and Disembark leave readiness unchanged and are forbidden while Spent. Recovery does not heal damage or grant another activation. Spent units still count for passes and objectives.

Reminders

Advance: Half current speed, rounded up, then add equipment bonuses. No general attack penalty. Ballistic recoil remains -1; Gunner’s limit is half-speed movement cost. Rockets use Fire only.

Split squad: Regroup before doing anything else. Move toward the chain, Hold if in place, or Disengage if engaged. No attack, repair, boarding, or exit. Clear readiness when this activation ends; regroup again next time if still split.

Passengers: Track separately from their carrier. Hold aboard clears readiness. Boarding, unloading, failed unloading, and forced evacuation do not. Spent passengers must Hold before unloading on a later activation.

Breakthrough: An unengaged group may exit with Move, Advance, or Rush, paying the action’s costs. A squad exits together. Regrouping cannot score an exit.
