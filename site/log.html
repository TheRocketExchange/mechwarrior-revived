What mattered

For the v0.3 comparison, record case A/B/C/D, fixed-model or repriced armies, and the formula used. Count attacks as rolls actually made, not orders declared. Record pushing damage separately from critical misses and enemy attacks.

After the return match

  • Where did conventional infantry help? Where were they ineffective?
  • Did anyone mistakenly give an ally an accuracy bonus?
  • Was transport useful? Did passengers get any unintended extra actions?
  • Did heat force pauses? Did different heat tracks feel different?
  • Did the same side or role win both games? Was that down to deployment, armies, players, or dice?
  • Which pilots helped? Did any feel essential or useless?
  • Which weapons did you choose, against what, and why? Did their costs affect your plans?
  • Lookups after round 2: __. Longest wait A/B: __. Game lengths: __.
  • One suggested change, why, and what it should improve: __.
  • Next step: keep / clarify / test again / try different points. Next game: __.
