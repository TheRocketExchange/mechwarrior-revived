Scenarios

Choose a scenario.

Start with First Contact at 600 points. The other three missions support all four game sizes: use 48 × 48 inches for Skirmish or Standard, and the 72 × 48 setup for Large or Grand Battles. Place terrain before choosing sides, then play again with roles or edges exchanged.
