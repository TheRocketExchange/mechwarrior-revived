The hosted rulebooks are official game texts. The handbook combines archived scenarios with community advice and changes. A separate fan replacement called “MechWarrior Revised” has not been confirmed.

The local handbook is 2,040,496 bytes. Its SHA-256 is 3E0822C0C44E61CE1F16AAFEB2C5A744232AD9475771912508528319DC03DC99. The public file has not been compared byte for byte.

What carries over

Use current dial values and printed ranges. Roll three dice plus attack against defense. The two white dice decide critical hits and misses. See Dark Age pp. 14–19.

Terrain bonuses, damage limits, heat timing, transports, pilots, and scoring in this packet are new Revived rules.

Using older figures

The equipment sheet covers six colors. Unlisted markings have no effect; note them during tests. Original named heat effects and circle-use costs do not apply.

Old symbols do not identify Rockets separately. Use fixed labels from an agreed source, or mark a Rocket test substitute. That label does not establish the figure’s original armament.

Revived replaces printed costs with one points formula. Its weights are playtest estimates, not published WizKids values.

Unit catalog and photographs

The catalog includes all 1,649 miniature entries in WarrenBorn’s full index: 1,644 figures and five DropShip turret sections. The 712 separate pilot, gear, and other card entries are outside this miniature catalog. Each datasheet links to its exact archive record and photograph. Photos are locally cached so the cards work offline; they retain their source credit.

Each collector variant keeps its own dial, photo, and cost. Quad ’Mechs use ’Mech rules. Gray Death Scout Armor and PAL suits use battle armor rules. Aircraft, aquatic units, colossal ’Mechs, artillery-only units, and DropShip sections are included for catalog completeness and marked as outside the core rules.

For old Ballistic symbols, only an unambiguous single-weapon missile/LRM/SRM unit name identifies a Rocket. Other weapons remain Ballistic unless documented otherwise. Purple and other unlisted combat colors remain visible on the datasheets but give no Revived equipment effect.

Cached source pages, the import manifest, and the coverage report are in units/. This establishes coverage of the archive index; it is not an independent audit of every promotional release. Check an archive discrepancy against the physical dial before playing.
