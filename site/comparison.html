Rules & handbook comparison

v0.3 · Playtest

Revived’s biggest changes are alternating activations and infantry that fight independently. Earlier rules also offer useful ideas for movement, heat, transport, and missions.

The sources

Age of Destruction is WizKids’ official revision of the clix game. A separate fan ruleset under “MechWarrior Revised” or “Age of Destruction (Updated Ruleset)” has not been identified. The comparison below uses the full Rules of Warfare, not its quick-start section.

The downloaded Scenario Handbook v2.1 is by Mike “Rimoran” McCarrig, dated December 15, 2020. Its 116 pages collect scenarios, writing advice, and suggested changes. It is a community compilation, not a replacement rulebook. Page references below use its printed numbers.

Rules compared

Topic Age of Destruction Revived v0.3
Activations One order per 150 build points each player turn Alternate groups; every group acts once per round
Infantry Temporary formations Fixed squads of 2–3 bases share one order; each keeps its own targets, rolls, and damage
Formation shooting Eligible supporters add +2 each to the main attacker Each base rolls its own attack; no ally accuracy bonus
Move and attack ’Mech assault permits half-speed shooting or full-speed close combat, with extra heat and a defense penalty Half-speed Advance shooting, with −1 only for Ballistic recoil. Move travels full speed without an attack. Infantry and ’Mechs Assault at half speed
Pushing and recovery Non-’Mechs normally take pushing damage for a second order token and cannot take orders with two tokens; a turn without an order clears tokens Combat orders commit, then push. Move or Hold recovers. Spent units must recover; they still contest and count for passes
Heat Printed profiles, pushing, cooling, and heat effects Printed tracks and numeric modifiers, heat per action, one stress rule for colored warnings
Transport Boarding and unloading use carrier movement; passengers are lost when it is destroyed Squad spends its activation; carrier acts separately. Passengers may survive a destroyed carrier
Victory Win a majority of three conditions: elimination, battlefield control, and enemy deployment zone Each scenario sets one mission and its scoring
Pilots Purchased cards modify ’Mechs Four roles; one pilot allowance included in each ’Mech’s cost; no advancement
Points Printed cost on each base One shared formula uses the full dial and Revived weapon, equipment, and heat rules
Weapons Energy, Ballistic, and Melee symbols Separate Rocket labels; recoil, heat, and indirect fire give different tradeoffs

AoD formation bonuses require a legal formation and line of sight; simple adjacency is not enough. AoD infantry can also capture units, and its victory rules already include objectives beyond kills.

Revived makes those choices simpler to follow: no combined attacks, and a clear mission for each scenario. More units acting each round may also make combat faster and deadlier.

Ideas from the handbook

These are summaries of the booklet, followed by possible uses in Revived. They are not claims that the new rules have been tested.

Entry What it offers Use in Revived
Writing a Scenario, pp. 3–5 Clear forces, setup, objectives, and victory; few special rules A consistent short format for the first missions
Urban Warfare, p. 64 Foot infantry can capture a factory and must hold it for three full turns; defender reinforcements arrive in stages Infantry doing mission work. Hold the Station uses consecutive round-end control
SC-005 Breaking The Line, p. 105 600 points face 900 with forward deployment; a unit must remain in the enemy deployment zone Breakthrough uses equal points and an escape target so one cheap runner is not enough
SC-003 Take The HPG Station, p. 104 Contact with the station may grant an extra attack; normal victory rules remain Use the station as the objective, without the extra attack
SC-008 King of the Hill, p. 105 Heat depends on actions rather than pushing/order tokens Keep printed heat tracks but change when heat is gained
SC-012 Fast and Furious, p. 106 Vehicles can move half speed and shoot, with order-token costs Revived v0.3 also uses half-speed Advance, with combat orders advancing readiness
SC-A03 Death March, p. 107 More non-’Mech orders, with attack/defense penalties instead of pushing damage A precedent for more active support units
M-009 Control the City and M-010 Flush ’em Out, p. 12 Missions involve reaching terrain and clearing cover Possible follow-ups to Seize and Hold

SC-001 Escape the City (p. 104) still uses normal victory rules. Despite its title, it is not an exit mission.

Choices for Revived

Keep alternating activations, separate infantry attacks, squads, printed attack dice, and printed heat tracks. Version 0.3 adds pushing, recovery, half-speed Advance, and a common squad order. See the worked examples for their effects and the test comparison. These adapt the original rhythm without copying its order budget or combined-fire bonuses.

The first missions test holding ground, escaping, and capturing a station. Score at round end so both players can respond. Old “per turn” effects need care: under alternating activations, they could trigger far more often.

Hold the Station lets any eligible unit contest. Infantry’s advantage is cover and access to fieldworks. If that proves too weak, test infantry-only capture later.

Leave weather, reserves, faction restrictions, captives, and extra station attacks for later. Test pilots separately from point changes. The handbook gives useful ideas; balance and play time still need games.
