About the rules

Design notes

v0.2 · Playtest

Revived brings existing MechWarrior clix figures into larger battles: 4–8 ’Mechs per side, with vehicles and infantry. The aim is a game of about two hours in which infantry matter for the ground they hold and the attacks they make.

Roles on the table

Unit Job Weakness
Conventional infantry Hold cover and objectives; assault exposed armor Vulnerable in the open; little damage against larger targets
Battle armor Fight vehicles and ’Mechs up close Must reach them; gains less from fieldworks
Vehicles Move and fire; carry infantry Cannot fight in close combat
’Mechs Bring firepower where it is needed Heat forces pauses; damage weakens the dial

What stays

Keep the figures, bases, ranges, arcs, combat values, and changing dials. Keep each ’Mech’s heat track and vent rating. Infantry in a squad still use their own values.

Attacks keep the original three dice and white-dice critical results. See the source notes.

What changes

Rule Purpose
Alternate one ’Mech, vehicle, or squad; everyone acts once per round Keep both players involved
Fixed squads of 2–3 infantry bases Reduce waiting through many cheap activations
Advance allows movement and shooting without a general attack penalty Encourage maneuver and mobile vehicle support
Each base attacks separately, without ally accuracy bonuses Let infantry earn their place through their own actions
Stronger infantry cover and close assaults against exposed armor Make terrain and timing matter
Heat comes from actions; Hold and Vent cool Fit heat to alternating activations
Printed heat modifiers and one stress roll for colored warnings Keep different heat tracks with fewer lookups
Six equipment colors with new meanings Use one reference across collections
Ballistic recoil, Energy heat, and stationary Rocket fire Give each weapon a strength and a cost
Up to two free carriers, each holding one squad Make transport easy to test with existing figures
Scenario-specific victory Keep attention on the mission
Four pilot roles, one included in each ’Mech’s cost Add character without advancement
One points formula for every unit Price the full dial under Revived’s rules

There are no campaigns, pilot levels, custom units, or expansion rules in this version.

What needs testing

Every unit acting each round may make fights much deadlier. Cheap squads may still let a player delay important activations. Check cover, transport survival, and two- versus three-base squads.

The shared points formula is a starting estimate. Test wider ranges of chassis and heat tracks after the first sample armies, then adjust the shared weights if results show a consistent problem.

Use the playtest plan, repeat games with sides reversed, and record time and rules lookups. Balance and the two-hour target remain untested.
