Army builder, cards, and dial sheets

Reading a unit card
  • Catalog ID identifies the exact release and variant, such as AOD108. Use it to find the same miniature in the catalog.
  • Roster ID is your own label for one figure: M1 for a ’Mech, V1 for a vehicle, C1a for an infantry base, or A1a for battle armor. It helps distinguish identical figures.
  • Faction is the allegiance of this exact catalog figure. A single-faction force uses only figures with that faction; Mixed factions allows any combination.
  • Squad records the infantry group, such as C1. Assigned carrier records its transport, such as V1. On a vehicle card, mark whether it is a carrier and record its assigned squad. Record whether passengers are aboard separately; leave unused fields blank.
  • Clicks is the damage needed to reach destruction from the starting position. It stays the same on the card; the current dial position tells you how much remains. Heat uses its own separate track.
  • Full combat dial shows every position in order. Damage moves right one column per point; repairs move left, subject to repair stops. Start is the starting position, Stop is a repair mark, and End is destroyed. X marks a lost value. SD on the heat dial means shutdown. Color letters keep equipment and heat warnings readable in black-and-white prints.

Photographs and original dial data: WarrenBorn’s MechWarrior archive. Every datasheet links to its source. Figures outside the current rules are marked; their calculated costs are estimates for later rules.
