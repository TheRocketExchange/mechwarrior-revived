# Pilot cards

Version 0.3

Give each ’Mech one pilot before deployment; its cost includes the pilot allowance. Each role is limited to 1 in Skirmish, 2 in Standard, 3 in Large Battle, or 4 in Grand Battle. See the [force chart](../rules/core-rules.md#force-organization). Write the ’Mech’s ID on its card.

[Print the cards](revived-pilot-cards.pdf) at 100% on US Letter paper. Each card is 2.5 × 3.5 inches. One sheet covers Skirmish; print up to two for Standard, three for Large Battle, or four for Grand Battle. [View the sheet](revived-pilot-cards-preview.png).

| Pilot | Ability |
| --- | --- |
| Gunner | During Advance, ignore Ballistic recoil if movement costs no more than half your speed, rounded up. Apply heat to speed first and count terrain costs. |
| Skirmisher | Take no damage when you Disengage. Follow the normal movement and contact rules. |
| Brawler | Add 1 close damage against a vehicle or ’Mech when attacking its rear or while it is shutdown. Equipment and pilot damage bonuses together cannot exceed +1. |
| Engineer | Vent cools 1 extra click, even while shutdown. Stop at the heat dial’s starting position. |

Abilities work whenever their conditions are met; there are no use counters. They affect only the assigned ’Mech and remain through combat-dial damage. Pilots cannot transfer and are lost when their ’Mech is destroyed. Only Engineer works during shutdown.

Gunner’s limit covers an ordinary half-speed Advance. If blue speed gives extra movement, stay within the half-speed cost to keep the benefit. Gunner removes recoil, not cover or heat, and cannot let Rockets fire during Advance. No pilot adds an attack or activation. Do not apply the same benefit twice: Skirmisher and gray speed both remove the same Disengage damage.

Use these cards without original pilots, gear, or advancement rules. Record their effects before changing the shared pilot allowance in the [points formula](../rules/points.md).
